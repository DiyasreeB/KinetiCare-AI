

\# KinetiCare AI — Limitations and Safety



\## Current Prototype

\- The application uses simulated movement signals only.

\- It does not connect to a real wearable sensor or use Bluetooth.

\- Movement metrics are basic estimates generated from synthetic data.

\- The session controls demonstrate application states, not real-time sensor monitoring.



\## Testing Limitations

\- Five basic automated tests have passed using synthetic signals.

\- These tests check simplified repetition-counting behavior.

\- They do not prove that the application is clinically accurate.

\- The complete application has not been clinically validated.



\## Safety and Privacy

\- This prototype is not a medical device.

\- It must not be used to diagnose conditions or make treatment decisions.

\- It does not replace a doctor or physiotherapist.

\- Only synthetic data should be used for the demonstration.



\## Future Improvements

\- Integrate a suitable wearable sensor and Bluetooth connection.

\- Test with real movement data under appropriate supervision and consent.

\- Improve signal processing and validate results against expert measurements.

\- Add stronger privacy protections and accessibility testing.
